# Review and approval

Read before building a review page, showing anything to the user for approval, or acting on a decision they send back.

## Rules

- **REV-01** A person approves before anything posts or schedules. Scheduling happens only on request. (judgment)
- **REV-02** An approval page works the moment it opens. Every approve, deny and note control is live from first paint and never waits on a sign-in or a save service. Picks keep on the device as they are tapped, go to the store when it answers, and a Copy my picks button hands back a plain list. Click it in a browser before sending the link.
- **REV-03** A new approval round shows only the videos that came back, each card with the reviewer's note and what changed. Earlier picks are cleared, and every preview is at a new link so a phone never replays a cached copy. (judgment)
- **REV-04** An approval is tied to the file. It is remembered per video id plus the filed file's size and time, so a re-delivered video starts unapproved.
- **REV-05** A review card per video shows the opening frame, key frames, format, hook look, and the gate result with its FIX and CHECK lines. It has Approve and Flag with a note, then the b-roll check: frames beside the words said over them, and a Wrong shot flag with a note. Copy decisions lists the flagged videos with their notes. (judgment)
- **REV-06** Fix each flagged video through the same chain (spec edit, gate, render, deliver), and post nothing until every video is approved. (judgment)
- **REV-07** Show a small slice first. Build and show 1 to 3 items that cover the variety, then fan out. Do the whole batch at once only when told. (judgment)
- **REV-13** Review copies the approver watches (footage review, hook and script approval) come from the raw footage with no grade. They carry the camera's real audio: AAC at 256 kbps made from the PCM and measured against it before a page is shown. Previews are cut tight and heard before the human sees them, with pauses of 0.45 s or more out and false starts out (CUT-11).
- **REV-14** Every automatic pick (format, hook look, caption style, music, b-roll) carries a one-line reason the reviewer can read. A field leaves the review page only when its default has been accepted about 95% of the time over three batches. (judgment)

## Optional: approving words first, and the learning loop

- **REV-08** Every send-back carries a reason code, so learning is real: cut-too-loose, cut-too-tight, clipped-word, wrong-hook, wrong-order, caption-size, caption-position, caption-timing, too-many-slams, too-few-slams, wrong-broll, missing-broll, music-wrong-feel, music-too-loud, audio-quality, overlay-text-wrong, other. (optional)
- **REV-09** Approve hooks and scripts before editing. The approver only cuts lines and never rewrites. An approved hook goes on screen as written, with no rewording and no comma fix. If it breaks a rule, the editor flags the rule and the line and lets the approver decide. (judgment) (optional)
- **REV-12** Learning loop: every pick, flip and note writes a row. A script tallies the rows and proposes one-line rules, and a human locks them. The skill's code never changes by itself, and a rule that has not fired in ten batches is flagged to retire. (optional)
