# B-roll

Read before choosing, placing, judging or generating any footage that plays over the speaker's words.

## Matching the words

- **BR-01** B-roll shows what the words say, or there is none. Literal beats related. Footage of the place or event under words about something else (context) fails, drone splits included. (judgment)
- **BR-02** The agent that placed the shots never judges them. A separate judge reads a sheet of each shot's first, last and punched-in frames beside the words spoken around it, and records PASS or FAIL with a reason. FAILs go back and the sheet is rebuilt. PASS means a viewer would agree, at every moment of the shot, that this is what is being talked about. (judgment)
- **BR-03** Judge the whole shot edge to edge: a pan that drifts onto something bad ends the shot early. End the shot when the words move on. The subject must read on a phone: no abstract macro, no sliver of a subject. (judgment)
- **BR-04** Never cut away from what the speaker is showing, holding or demonstrating, because the A-roll is the b-roll. Process words need the matching step of the process: the shot for setup is not the shot for the run. (judgment)
- **BR-05** Real footage first. With no match in the library, stay on the speaker, or use an AI image or clip of an object or place the words name. Never people, and never a stand-in for a real client, place or person. A talk video that stays on the speaker is not a defect. Only a clip reused across videos is. (judgment)
- **BR-06** Someone sitting with a phone is never the b-roll for using one. When the words are about seeing an ad or going through someone's content, show the real screen, such as a live profile captured and scrolled. (judgment)
- **BR-07** One visual language per video, in your own light and palette. Each shot lands on its phrase and is held long enough to read, with the speaker back on screen for the key lines. (judgment)

## Footage quality and reuse

- **BR-08** Clean footage only: no settling wobble at the start, focus hunting, bump, someone walking through, or camera swinging off at the end. Fail on jitter over 9 px (after removing smooth moves), a jolt over 60 px, a first 0.6 s moving 3.5x the rest, or a quarter of the frames out of focus. Motion is read at the top and bottom of the frame and the calmer one wins. A deliberate handheld follow can be signed off with a true reason.
- **BR-09** One shot per batch. The same clip twice in a video, or the same moment of a clip in two videos of a batch, fails. A different moment of a long take is a CHECK.

## Where b-roll comes from

- **BR-10** B-roll comes only from clips the user marked Keep in the review, and only inside their kept parts when trimmed. Cut and unreviewed clips are never placed. When no kept clip fits, stay on the speaker or ask. New footage goes onto the review page before it is used. (judgment)
- **BR-11** The user's own drops for a video go first (a file named after the words pins it there). The library fills the gaps, and every drop is judged like any other shot. (judgment)
- **BR-12** Real elements are real footage. Fire, water, smoke or sky behind a page or a cut is filmed footage or AI-generated video looped seamlessly, never a coded shader or a CSS imitation. Check the user's shoots and past assets first. (judgment)

## Treatment

- **BR-13** B-roll is silent, with no shot sound and no beds, unless a reason is recorded. The voice, the music bed and any clicks carry it. (judgment)
- **BR-14** Footage shown for context is a faded overlay feathered into the frame, not a framed tile or a phone. A device appears only where the scene explains someone using it. (judgment)
- **BR-15** A still that carries a scene never just sits under a slow push with labels popping on. What is in it moves when it is named. Cut it into layers (object masks, a person matte, an empty plate behind) so the named object comes toward camera, the rest steps back, and type can travel behind the subject. If the only motion is the camera plus pop-on labels, it fails. (judgment)
- **BR-16** Horizontal clips are centre-cropped to 9:16, so pick clips whose subject survives the crop, or reframe with a zoom origin. Montage shots run 1.5 to 4 s, one idea per shot, and something new still lands at least every 2.5 s (GFX-04).

## Fit and safety

- **BR-17** Brand and people checks apply to b-roll. Flag readable customer names or art that is not cleared, someone who could be taken for the owner or another named person, and anything unflattering (a rude gesture in a mural, a masked face). Licence plates are flagged and covered at render, with a person confirming. Never name people from how they look. (judgment)
- **BR-18** AI imagery is objects and environments only, with no faces. Look at it before use and record a reason for each AI clip. Whether to generate fill b-roll at all is the human's call per video, and it is off by default on a first batch. (judgment)
